Plugin types: [Script Writers](../plugin-types/script-writers/).

**Script Aid** is the fuller of FilmOpen's two screenwriting plugins: a team that develops a story with you and writes the screenplay into the project. A run of a pilot costs about USD 40 at the measure taken when it was built, depending on your models. Its lighter sibling is [Script Aid Lite](../fo-script-aid-lite/).

## The team

A **showrunner**, a **story architect** and a **character developer** plan the story. **One actor per principal character** rehearses the key scenes, knowing only what that character knows at that point in the story. **One screenwriter** writes every scene. **Independent reviewers** read each scene: character, dialogue, dramaturgy, continuity and facts, the audience, and a blind judge.

All of the story's memory lives in your project, so you can leave and come back.

## Use it

1. [Install and allow](../plugin-packages/#install-and-allow) **Script Aid** in **Settings → Plugins**.
2. Open your project's **Workflow** tab. Each row runs one step on the project, folder or scene the tab is open on, with what you type into it.
3. Each step ends in a proposal. Review it and press **Apply**, or leave it.

Each step asks for a text model on a platform you have set up: see [the platforms](/docs/platforms/).
