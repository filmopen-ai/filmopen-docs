Plugin types: [Prompt Builders](../plugin-types/prompt-builders/).

**fo-styles v2** (Style Builder) offers 50 filming and visual treatment styles in five categories. It needs FilmOpen API 1, revision 11.

- **Modern Cinema**, 12 styles
- **Classic Cinema**, 10 styles
- **Cell Phone**, 10 styles
- **Animated**, 10 styles
- **Experimental Cinema**, 8 styles

Each style has a title, a description, the words it adds to a prompt and a picture of the same scene in that style. The [Style Guides](../style-guides/) show every style with its picture and words.

## Choose a style

[Install and allow](../plugin-packages/#install-and-allow) **fo-styles** in **Settings → Plugins**. It needs no key and no setup. On the project's or a folder's **Visual treatment** card, open the **Style** picker; it reads **Select Style** while none is chosen. A tile's **Show full size** icon opens its full view, where **Use this** chooses the style and **Copy** copies its prompt.

The words, the titles and the small pictures come with the package. The full-size pictures are online, so showing one needs a connection; the plug-in itself fetches nothing and renders nothing.
