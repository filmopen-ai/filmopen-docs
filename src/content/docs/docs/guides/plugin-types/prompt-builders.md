A prompt builder declares a catalogue of entries that FilmOpen shows, each with its own words. The words of the entry you choose are added after your own prompt. A prompt builder renders nothing and needs no key. The [Style Guides](../../style-guides/) show every entry with its picture.

## Subtypes

- Visual style catalogue to render prompt — implemented
