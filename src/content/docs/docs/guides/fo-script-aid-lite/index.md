Plugin types: [Script Writers](../plugin-types/script-writers/).

**Script Aid Lite** is the lighter of FilmOpen's two screenwriting plugins: one writer in five roles, story editor, architect, writer, reader and revision writer. It develops your concept and writes the screenplay into the project, one scene at a time. A run of a pilot costs about USD 20 at the measure taken when it was built. Its heavier sibling is [Script Aid](../fo-script-aid/).

## Use it

1. [Install and allow](../plugin-packages/#install-and-allow) **Script Aid Lite** in **Settings → Plugins**.
2. Open your project's **Workflow** tab. Steps run on the project, folder or scene the tab is open on.
3. **Tell the story**, then **Create folders here** in your own words (*three seasons*, *five episodes*, *two acts*), **Refine the synopsis**, **Describe the scenes**, and on a scene **Write the scene** and **Change the scene**.
4. Each step ends in a proposal. Review it and press **Apply**.

**Write the scene** keeps your quoted lines word for word. A speaker you name who isn't a character yet becomes one. **Change the scene** works on the blocks the project holds now, so your own edits are what it changes.

Each step uses a text model on a platform you have set up: see [the platforms](/docs/platforms/).
